CAREER ADAPTABILITY REFLECTION

How are you preparing for your future?

Respond to two statements in each dimension. This reflection supports planning; it is not a psychological test or a predictor of employment.

Pathly wrote these eight statements based on four career adaptability dimensions. This is not the 24-item CAAS instrument and has not been psychometrically validated.

CONCERN

How much do you think about and prepare for your future?

I have a sense of my career goals for the next 6–12 months.
I have identified actions I need to take soon.
CONTROL

How actively do you guide your own decisions and actions?

I make career decisions based on my own judgment.
I keep taking small steps even when I do not feel fully ready.
CURIOSITY

How actively do you explore and compare possibilities?

I look for information about tasks and skills in different careers.
I compare options before choosing my main target.
CONFIDENCE

How confident are you in learning and overcoming obstacles?

I believe I can learn skills I have not mastered yet.
When I face difficulties, I can find another approach and try again.
